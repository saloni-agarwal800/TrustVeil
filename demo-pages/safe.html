Growing tomatoes on a balcony

Tomatoes do well in pots as long as they get six hours of sun. Pick a container that holds at least ten litres of soil and has drainage holes at the bottom.

Water deeply every two or three days instead of a little each day. Feed the plants every few weeks once flowers appear, and stake the stems before they get heavy with fruit.

Small varieties such as cherry tomatoes are the easiest place to start, and they fruit earlier than the large ones.
