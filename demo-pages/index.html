TrustVeil demo pages

These pages are made up for testing the extension. Nothing here is real and nothing is submitted anywhere.

  • Fake free-trial page: pre-ticked box, Rs 1 then Rs 999/month, countdown, tiny "no thanks".
  • Fake bank login: claims to be a bank, asks for a password, no HTTPS.
  • Normal article: should show no warning.

For the look-alike address demo, add 127.0.0.1 sbi-secure-login.com to your hosts file and open http://sbi-secure-login.com:5000/fake-bank.html.
